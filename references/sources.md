# Sources and how to use them

These sources inform editorial judgement and document techniques. Apply them selectively for the paper, discipline, and venue; their examples and numerical prescriptions are not universal requirements. References are provided for guidance, not for automatic insertion into manuscripts using the skill.

## Communicating the paper's idea

- **Brett Mensh and Konrad Kording (2017), "Ten simple rules for structuring papers."** [PLOS Computational Biology](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005619), 13(9), e1005619. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005619. Editorial guidance on a clear contribution, sufficient context, logical progression, interpretation, and attention to titles, abstracts, and figures.
- **Simon Peyton Jones, "How to Write a Great Research Paper."** [Author's page and slides](https://simon.peytonjones.org/great-research-paper/). Supports explicit ideas and contributions, concrete examples, fair treatment of prior work, and reader feedback. Its page counts and categorical style advice are contextual.

## Presentation and LaTeX

- **W3C WAI, headings and PDF structure.** [Descriptive headings](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html), [PDF9: heading tags](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/pdf/PDF9), [PDF3: reading order](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/pdf/PDF3), and [PDF6: table markup](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/pdf/PDF6). Supports navigable, meaningful structure and describes accessible PDF techniques; it does not prescribe research-paper headings or certify a document's accessibility.
- **Ann Catrine Eldh, Liselott Årestedt, and Carina Berterö (2020), "Quotations in Qualitative Studies: Reflections on Constituents, Custom, and Purpose."** [International Journal of Qualitative Methods](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1609406920969268). DOI: 10.1177/1609406920969268. Methodological discussion of deliberately using quotations to illuminate analysis or findings. It does not establish that quotations or visual prominence automatically validate findings or make readers trust a paper.
- **Bridget C. O'Brien et al. (2014), "Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research: A Synthesis of Recommendations."** [Academic Medicine](https://doi.org/10.1097/ACM.0000000000000388), 89(9), 1245-1251. Item S17 connects quotations and other excerpts to analytic findings. This supports making the relationship between participant accounts and analysis clear, while allowing flexible report organization.
- **Boris Veytsman, official `acmart` manual.** [LaTeX Class for the Association for Computing Machinery](https://mirrors.ctan.org/macros/latex/contrib/acmart/acmart.pdf). The consulted version was 2.21, dated 1 November 2026. Describes class-supported figures, tables, image descriptions, tagging, and publication styling. Match instructions to the installed class and current venue rather than assuming all LaTeX classes share these commands.
- **LaTeX Project, accessible PDF documentation.** [Usage instructions](https://latex3.github.io/tagging-project/documentation/usage-instructions) and [class/package tagging status](https://latex3.github.io/tagging-project/tagging-status/). Supports version-aware tagging, image alternatives, and table headers. Check current compatibility when changing the toolchain; a compiler or metadata change is not a universal prerequisite for every layout edit.

## Further reading on AI-assisted summaries

- **Uwe Peters and Benjamin Chin-Yee (2025), "Generalization bias in large language model summarization of scientific research."** [Royal Society Open Science](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12042776/), 12(4), 241776. DOI: 10.1098/rsos.241776. Documents scope changes in many model-generated summaries relative to their sources.
- **Hye Sun Yun et al. (2025), "Caught in the Web of Words: Do LLMs Fall for Spin in Medical Literature?"** [CHIL 2025, PMLR 287](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v287/yun25a.html), 458-479. Examines how positive framing in medical-trial literature can influence model interpretations and summaries.

These experiments motivate checking meaning when revising scientific claims; their tested tasks and models do not describe every writing situation. Keep that verification proportionate to the actual edit.
